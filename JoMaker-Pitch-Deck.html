The Problem

The Jordanian market has a real gap

🔍
Clients Can't Find Workshops
No trusted directory for digital manufacturing workshops exists. Discovery relies entirely on personal connections.
❓
Zero Price Transparency
Every client gets a different quote. No comparison, no fair-price guarantee, no standard benchmark.
🏭
Workshops Lack Reach
Idle production capacity. Skilled workshops cannot access clients beyond their immediate network.
📈
Soaring Raw Material Costs
Small workshops buy materials at retail prices — they lack the volume to negotiate wholesale. This inflates production costs and kills competitiveness against imports.
💡
The Result
Clients turn to expensive imports — while capable local workshops lose business they could have won.

Unique Feature

Group Buying — nothing like it locally

🤝
A Simple but Powerful Idea
Multiple workshops join a shared purchasing campaign for the same raw material — the more members, the lower the unit price.
📊
Smart Deposit System
Workshops pay 50% upfront as a deposit to confirm commitment. The remaining 50% is collected once the minimum quantity is reached.
🏭
The Power of the Group
✅ Wholesale pricing for workshops of any size
✅ Lower production costs, higher profit margins
✅ Increases workshop loyalty to the platform
